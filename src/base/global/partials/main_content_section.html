The Apex

Apex Monsters are a unique categorization in Monster Hunter Wilds. While the title itself doesn't translate to anything necessarily special, it does denote the increased difficulty and thereby increased loot of these monsters. We'll tell you which are the apex monsters of which regions and exactly what that means here.

These monsters will be naturally encountered through the story and are a bit harder than your average monster. However, they appear quite frequently in the end game and can appear as their Tempered variants just like any other monster.

The most important thing to keep in mind about the Apex Monsters is that they generally have better equipment than other monsters, excluding some end-game monsters. They also inhabit a unique Rarity 6 slot when they're encountered as Tempered Monsters, allowing them to drop the elusive Hunter's Symbol III.
